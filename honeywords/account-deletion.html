Account deletion

Email tupitupi to request permanent deletion of your Honey Words account and its associated cloud saves. You can make a request even if you’ve uninstalled the game.

Email an account deletion request

This opens a draft. Review it and send it from your email app.

You can also email tupitupigames@gmail.com with the subject Honey Words account deletion. Include your game username and player ID if you know them. Never include your password or sign-in codes.

What happens next

  1. Send your request and tell us which Honey Words account you want deleted.
  2. We may ask for more information to verify that the account belongs to you.
  3. We’ll confirm by email when the deletion is complete.

What gets deleted

We delete your Honey Words account and its associated cloud saves, including saved puzzle progress. Deleted account data and cloud progress cannot be restored. Records may need to be kept for legal obligations, security, or disputes.

Clear data on your device

  1. Android. Open Settings, find Honey Words under Apps, then choose Storage and Clear storage. Menu names may vary by device.
  2. iPhone or iPad. In Settings, go to General, then iPhone or iPad Storage. Choose Honey Words and tap Delete App.

Clearing app data removes progress and settings from that device. To remove your account and cloud saves, send the email request above. Device backups may keep separate copies.

Delete information you emailed us

If you shared personal information with support, email us to request deletion. Tell us which correspondence the request is about. Keep passwords out of your message.
