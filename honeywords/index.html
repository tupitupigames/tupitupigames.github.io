Help the bees
bottle their honey

The bees have a fresh batch of honey and a row of empty bottles. Swipe through the honeycomb to spell a word. The tiles tumble, the honey flows, and it’s time to fill another bottle.

See how it works ↓

A few words for the bees

Start with a word you can see. Once its tiles drop away, the letters left behind might give you your next one.

Daily

Work through the honeycomb one word at a time and see if you can clear the board.

Infinite

Keep finding words as new letters fall in, and see how high you can score.

The boards

In Daily, every tile has somewhere to go. In Infinite, another letter is always on its way.
