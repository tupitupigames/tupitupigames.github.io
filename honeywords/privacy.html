Privacy policy

Updated 28 November 2026

We’re tupitupi, the team behind Honey Words. This policy explains how we handle information when you play, visit this site, or get in touch.

Game progress and settings

When you come back to a puzzle, Honey Words uses your saved progress to pick up where you left off. It also remembers preferences such as sound and vibration. If you use a Honey Words account, we use your player identifier to link your account to your cloud save. Cloud saves keep a copy of supported game records, such as puzzle progress, so you can sync and restore them. When available, the game checks the device advertising ID locally to determine whether to show an optional game mode.

Ads on Android

Honey Words uses Google AdMob for banner and rewarded ads. Google can collect and share your IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Your IP address may be used to estimate your general location. Google encrypts data in transit.

Use the game's ad privacy options when available to change your choices. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy for details and controls.

Website and email

This site has no sign-in, tracking scripts or contact form. The hosting provider may process technical information to serve and protect the site. If you email us about a puzzle, a bug, or a privacy question, we use your email address and what you tell us to reply and work through the issue.

Retention and deletion

Account and cloud-save records are kept while your account is active, unless they are removed through a verified deletion request or an applicable retention process. Progress and settings stored on your device stay there until you clear them. Device backups may keep a separate copy. We keep support correspondence as needed to handle your request and meet legal obligations. Google handles ad data under its own policy.

You can request deletion of your Honey Words account and associated cloud saves by email. See the account deletion page for what to include and how to clear data on your device. To ask about personal data you shared with us, contact tupitupi.
