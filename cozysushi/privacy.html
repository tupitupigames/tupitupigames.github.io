Cozy Sushi privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Cozy Sushi is an Android game provided by tupitupi, based in Indonesia ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains how information is handled when you play the game or contact us.

For privacy questions or requests, contact tupitupigames@gmail.com.

1. Information we handle

Account information. The current Android version requires an account to play. We use Unity Authentication to process your chosen username and password and assign a player identifier. The game stores session credentials on your device so you can remain signed in; it does not store your password locally. Please avoid putting your real name or other sensitive information in your username.

Game progress. We store your restaurant layout, staff and ingredient selections, progress, virtual currencies, unlocked items, tutorial progress, settings, and simulation records on your device. Unity Cloud Save stores a copy of supported game records associated with your player identifier so your progress can be synchronized and restored. Save records include timestamps and synchronization information. Local recovery copies may be created when saves are migrated or restored.

Optional analytics. If you allow analytics, Unity Analytics processes gameplay and technical information to help us understand and improve the game. This includes play sessions, tutorial and mission progress, restaurant upgrades, virtual currency activity, rewards, and game performance, such as frame times, memory use, and screen resolution. Unity also processes identifiers and network information, including your IP address, an installation identifier, and your Unity Authentication player identifier when signed in. These records may be associated with identifiers; they are not necessarily anonymous.

Advertising information. We use Google AdMob to show advertisements, including ads associated with in-game rewards. Depending on your privacy choices, device settings, and region, Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses, approximate location derived from IP addresses, advertising and other device identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. These services use information to deliver and measure ads, personalize ads where permitted, and detect fraud. Declining our optional Unity Analytics does not disable Google's separate advertising-related processing.

Network requests. The game contacts an online time service at worldtime.timezone.io to calculate time-dependent features such as offline earnings. It also checks an update file hosted on GitHub Pages. These requests expose your IP address and ordinary network request information to the relevant provider. The game does not intentionally send your account password or restaurant save to these endpoints.

Support messages. If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. When a signed-in player prepares an account deletion email, the game may create a verification code in a separate Unity Cloud Save support record and include it in the draft email with their username and player identifier. This helps us verify ownership. Preparing a draft does not submit a request or delete anything; you must send the email. Do not send your password.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide account access, save and restore your restaurant, synchronize progress, calculate game rewards, check for required updates, deliver and measure advertising, prevent misuse, respond to support and privacy requests, and meet legal obligations. With your permission, we use analytics to understand gameplay and improve performance and game balance.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on the basis appropriate to the activity: providing the service you request for essential account and save features; consent for optional analytics and advertising processing where required; legitimate interests, where permitted, for security and support; and compliance with legal obligations. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

3. Who receives information

We use Unity for authentication, cloud saves, and optional analytics; Google and its advertising partners for advertising; GitHub Pages for update-file hosting; and worldtime.timezone.io for online time checks. Our email provider processes support correspondence. Providers may process information on our behalf or for their own purposes as described in their policies. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to address fraud, security incidents, or legal claims.

4. Your choices and deletion requests

Analytics. Open PRIVACY on the welcome screen to allow or decline analytics. The DELETE ANALYTICS button requests deletion of Unity Analytics data and disables further optional analytics collection. This button does not delete your game account, cloud save, or advertising-provider data.

Advertising. Where available, use AD CHOICES from the privacy panel to review Google's advertising choices. You can also use your Android device's advertising privacy controls. Available options depend on your device, region, and Google's consent requirements. Ads that are not personalized may still involve data processing for delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Account and cloud data. Open PRIVACY on the welcome screen and choose REQUEST ACCOUNT DELETION to prepare an email, or email tupitupigames@gmail.com directly with the subject "Cozy Sushi account deletion" and your game username or player identifier, if available. You can also use the instructions at https://tupitupigames.github.io/cozysushi/account-deletion.html without installing or signing in to the game. We may need to verify that the account belongs to you. Never send your password. We aim to complete verified requests within 7 days, or sooner where required by law, and confirm completion by email. If more time is needed and legally permitted, we will explain why and provide an updated timeframe. Deletion covers your game account, associated cloud saves, and the verification record; we also address associated analytics through the relevant provider process. Deleted account and cloud progress cannot be restored through the game. Signing out or uninstalling does not itself delete server-held records.

Local information. You can remove local game data through Android's app storage controls. Local recovery copies can remain until the app's stored data is removed. Device or platform backups may retain copies under your backup settings. Local data removal does not itself delete your account, cloud save, or provider-held analytics and advertising information.

Other rights. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your information; restrict or object to certain processing; withdraw consent; or complain to a competent data protection authority. Contact us to make a request. Advertising partners' policies explain their additional privacy controls and request procedures.

5. Retention

Local game records remain until removed through device controls or replaced through ordinary gameplay and saving. Local migration and recovery backups are not automatically deleted by the game.

Account and cloud-save records remain while your account is maintained, unless deleted through a verified request or another applicable service retention process. A support verification code is valid for 30 days; its record is replaced when a new code is prepared after expiry or removed with your account deletion. Code expiry does not automatically remove the stored record. Unity states that Analytics personal data is retained for 13 months by default; the period for this game depends on the service configuration and valid deletion requests. Advertising, hosting, and other providers apply their own retention policies. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and related issues; unnecessary account identifiers and verification codes are removed after the request is resolved, unless a legal obligation requires retention.

Some information may need to be retained for legal obligations, security, or disputes. If an exception applies to your deletion request, we will explain the reason and applicable retention period. Deleted information may remain temporarily in provider backups until those backups expire under the provider's procedures.

6. Security and international processing

The game uses service authentication and HTTPS connections for the online features described above. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Keep your password private and protect access to your device.

We are based in Indonesia. Our providers may process information in other countries, where privacy laws may differ. Where required by applicable law, international transfers must be supported by appropriate safeguards.

7. Children

Cozy Sushi is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their personal information. Where local law requires a higher age or parental authorization, those requirements also apply. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where required.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the game or its services change. The published policy will show its latest revision date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent before a material change takes effect.
