Your own little
sushi shop

Pull up a stool and watch the first plates make their way around the belt. In Cozy Sushi, you get to decide where the tables go, who works in the kitchen, and what’s on the menu.

Get it on Google Play ↗

Make the shop yours

Start with a spot for the sushi counter. As the dining room takes shape, you can move things around, try a different menu, and see how the next service goes.

Lay out the shop

Put the sushi stations close to the belt and find a good spot for each table. You can shuffle the room around as your shop grows.

Hire staff

Bring in some helping hands for the kitchen and dining room. While the staff get the next plates ready, you can think about what to serve next.

Plan the menu

Choose what goes on the belt, then watch what guests pick up. Try a new recipe when you feel like changing things up.

A few plates from the kitchen

There’s salmon nigiri with a slice of salmon over rice, tuna maki wrapped in nori, and ikura gunkan topped with bright orange roe.
