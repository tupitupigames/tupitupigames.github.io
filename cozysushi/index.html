Privacy policy

Account & data requests

Delete your Cozy Sushi account

Email tupitupi to request permanent deletion of your game account and its associated cloud save. You can make your request here even if you have uninstalled the game.

Email a deletion request

This opens an email draft. Review it and press Send in your email app. No request is sent by this webpage.

No email app available? Send an email from any email service to tupitupigames@gmail.com with the subject Cozy Sushi account deletion.

Include your game username and your player ID if you know it. Never include your password, sign-in token, or device backup.

What happens next

  1. Send your request. Tell us which Cozy Sushi account you want deleted.
  2. Verify ownership. We may ask for additional account information so we do not delete someone else's account. If you still have the game, use PRIVACY → REQUEST ACCOUNT DELETION on the welcome screen. When available, it includes a verification code in your draft. You can still request deletion without that code, without reinstalling, and without signing in through this website.
  3. Receive confirmation. We aim to complete verified requests within 7 days, or sooner where required by law. If more time is needed and legally permitted, we will explain why and give an updated timeframe. We confirm completion by email.

What gets deleted

Your Unity Authentication account for Cozy Sushi, including its username and player identity; associated cloud saves containing restaurant progress, layouts, staff, virtual currencies and unlocked items; and the support verification record. We also address associated analytics data through the relevant provider process.

Deleted account and cloud progress cannot be restored through the game. This request does not delete your Google account or unrelated accounts you use with other games.

Local data, providers & limited retention

Deleting the cloud account does not remotely erase the local restaurant and recovery copies on your device. You can remove those through Android's app storage controls. Your device backup settings may retain separate copies. Uninstalling alone does not submit an account deletion request.

Some records may need to be retained for legal obligations, security, or disputes. If an exception applies to your request, we will explain which records are retained, why, and for how long. Provider backups may retain deleted information temporarily until their normal expiry; expiry periods depend on the provider.

We retain support correspondence as needed to resolve your request and related issues, removing unnecessary account identifiers and verification codes after resolution unless legally required to retain them. Advertising providers handle their own records and requests under their privacy policies. See our privacy policy for providers and privacy controls.

Only want to stop analytics?

In the game, open PRIVACY on the welcome screen to decline analytics or use DELETE ANALYTICS to request deletion of Unity Analytics data. That action is separate from deleting your account and cloud save.
